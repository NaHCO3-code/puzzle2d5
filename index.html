2.5D Puzzle

What's this?

2.5D Puzzle 是一个益智小游戏，灵感来源于正六边形一些良好的性质。

有趣的是，尽管这是一个平面图形，但是它看上去就像是一个由多个小正方体堆叠形成的立方体。

你可以点击三个相邻并且组成一个正六边形的菱形，将这个正六边形旋转。

进行一次旋转操作后，看上去就像是拿走或放上了一个小正方体！

游戏目标是使得所有“立方体”看上去都被拿走了，并且底面所有菱形颜色相同，侧面的每一层颜色相同。当然，你也可以拼接出任意你喜欢的图形。

祝你玩得开心！

2.5D Puzzle is a puzzle game that uses some good properties of regular hexagons.

But something interesting is that although it is a plane figure, it looks like a cube formed by stacking multiple small cubes.

You can click on three adjacent rhombuses that form a regular hexagon to rotate the hexagon.

After rotating a hexagon, it looks like you take away or put back one small cube!

The goal of the game is to make all "cubes" look like they have been taken away, and the colors of the bottom all rhombuses are the same, and each layer of the side faces is the same color.

Have fun!
